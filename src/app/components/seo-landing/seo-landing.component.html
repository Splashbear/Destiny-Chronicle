Destiny 2 Activity History Tracker

Destiny Chronicle is a Destiny 1 and Destiny 2 activity history tracker that turns your Bungie PGCR data into a searchable journal of every raid, dungeon, and match you have played.

What is Destiny activity history?

Destiny activity history is the complete record of your fireteam's Post Game Carnage Reports (PGCRs) — every strike, raid, dungeon, Crucible match, and playlist run. Destiny Chronicle pulls this data from the official Bungie API and lets you browse it by date so you can answer questions like:

  • "What was I doing in Destiny on this date years ago?"
  • "When was my first clear of each raid or dungeon?"
  • "How much time have I really spent in Destiny activities?"

Destiny Chronicle vs other Destiny tools

Destiny Chronicle is designed to complement, not replace, existing Destiny tools you might already know:

  • Destiny Heatmap and Scrubland / Scrublanddeux focus on calendar style playtime heatmaps.
  • Time Wasted on Destiny is great for a quick "how much time have I spent" snapshot.
  • Chrisfried's tools and other community sites offer powerful visualizations.

Destiny Chronicle concentrates on daily PGCR history and Guardian Firsts — a detailed journal of activities across Destiny 1 and Destiny 2, organized by date, year and mode with raid and dungeon firsts highlighted.

Features of the Destiny activity history view

  • Browse your Destiny 2 activity history by specific calendar date across multiple years.
  • See Destiny 1 and Destiny 2 activities side by side, grouped by raid, dungeon, and playlist.
  • View Guardian Firsts such as first ever activity, first raid clears, and first dungeon solos.
  • Export activity history, firsts, and titles to CSV/Excel for personal archives.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see my Destiny 2 activity history?

Open Destiny Chronicle, search for your Bungie name or membership ID, choose your platform, and select a date. The app will fetch your Bungie PGCR activity history for all linked characters and show everything you played on that day across Destiny 1 and Destiny 2.

Does Destiny Chronicle support Destiny 1?

Yes. Destiny Chronicle was built to preserve Destiny 1 activity history as well as Destiny 2. D1 raids and activities appear alongside D2 content for the same calendar date.

Is this affiliated with Time Wasted on Destiny or Destiny Heatmap?

No. Destiny Chronicle is a standalone fan project built on the Bungie API. It is inspired by community tools like Time Wasted on Destiny, Destiny Heatmap, and Scrublanddeux, but focuses on detailed per-date activity history and Guardian Firsts.
